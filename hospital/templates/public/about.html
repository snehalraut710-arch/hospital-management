About MediQueue

MediQueue is an AI powered hospital appointment and queue management system. It exists to remove two everyday frustrations: not knowing which specialist to see, and not knowing how long you will be waiting once you arrive.

How the AI works

The symptom checker runs entirely on this machine - there is no external API and no internet connection required. It combines two layers:

  • A machine learning classifier (word and character TF-IDF features into logistic regression) trained on a labelled dataset of symptom descriptions.
  • A curated knowledge base of weighted symptom phrases, including red-flag rules that override the model outright for emergency presentations.

Every recommendation shows its confidence and the words that drove it, so you can see why it suggested what it did. The assistant names a department, never a disease.

This is a student project built for educational purposes. It is not a medical device and must not be used for real clinical decisions.
